# ChimiaLabs — inspectable copper-ordering hypotheses

**OSI Hackathon 2026 · private-code research submission**

Which atomic arrangements can diffraction data distinguish? ChimiaLabs connects structural hypotheses, site-linked Truchet-style representations, native diffraction calculations and explicit comparison checks. The interactive research explorer lets a scientist follow a periodic arrangement through the crystal and inspect how a binary occupation relates to its translation average.

The workflow separates three questions: whether a structural representation is internally consistent, whether forward calculators agree for the same model, and whether measured data discriminate between candidate arrangements. An attractive pattern, numerical convergence or a lower residual does not by itself establish a unique physical ordering.
